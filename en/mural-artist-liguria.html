What kind of mural works well in Liguria ?

In Liguria, trompe-l’oeil facades, windows and cornices included, because the region has a long tradition of them. Everything is painted by hand in acrylic, with no wallpaper and no printing.

Does it damage the wall ?

No, the paint goes onto the plaster already in place. Dusting it down is usually enough. If the surface has cracks or rising damp, Giulia flags it before quoting.

When can we paint in Liguria ?

All year indoors. Outdoors, wait for a dry spell, the wall has to be perfectly dry.

Can I see the design before saying yes ?

Always. You get a sketch mounted on the photo of your wall, to scale, with the quote. Nothing is committed until you confirm, and revisions are included.

Murals shaped for Liguria

Liguria is a narrow strip between sea and mountain, where houses are tall, packed together and often built on the slope. The region has a long tradition of trompe-l'oeil painted facades, windows and cornices included, so clients here know what wall painting is. Salt and damp call for careful preparation before any colour. Giulia is right at the border, in Menton. Giulia travels easily to paint: she comes on site to see the wall, or works first from photos and measurements.

Frequently asked questions about murals in Liguria

How much does a mural cost in Liguria ?

The most requested formats are listed above, starting at 450 €. A small mural in a bedroom costs less than a facade that needs preparation and scaffolding. The quote is free and without obligation.

How long does it take ?

A simple wall takes two to three days of painting. A full room or a very detailed design takes around a week. Add a few days for the sketch and approval.

Does a mural last outdoors in Liguria ?

Yes, with surface preparation and paints made for outdoor use. Liguria has a long tradition of painted facades. Exterior work needs careful preparation against salt and damp.

Is permission needed to paint a facade ?

For an interior wall, no. For a facade visible from the street, you usually need the owner's or the building association's approval, and a notice to the town hall if the building is in a protected area. Giulia explains the process before starting.

Can a mural be changed or removed later ?

Yes. A mural painted in acrylic can be covered like any wall paint. It can also be reworked or extended later.
