Giulia Dal Bon is a painter creating hand-painted murals, drawings, portraits, landscapes, and graphic works for hotels, restaurants, boutiques, homes, and events across Monaco, Milan, the French Riviera, Liguria, Lombardy, and Piedmont, with projects considered elsewhere in Italy and France.
