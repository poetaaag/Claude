Milan · Cultural project

Mural dedicated to Gaber and Jannacci, Circolo Cerizza

A twenty-two-meter mural hand-painted by Giulia Dal Bon at Circolo Cerizza in Milan, in zone 2.

The project

Giulia Dal Bon painted a twenty-two-meter mural on the walls of Circolo Cerizza, Via Meucci 4, in Milan. The subject pays tribute to Giorgio Gaber and Enzo Jannacci, 2 major figures in twentieth-century Milanese culture.

The mural was painted entirely by hand. Its scale, composition, and cultural weight make it one of the strongest public projects in Giulia’s body of work.

Project details

  • Type : cultural mural, public artwork
  • Scale : 22 linear meters
  • Place : Circolo Cerizza, Via Meucci 4, Milan
  • Area : Milan zone 2
  • Technique : hand painted mural painting
  • Subject : Giorgio Gaber and Enzo Jannacci
  • Artist : Giulia Dal Bon, trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera

Who is Giulia Dal Bon

Giulia Dal Bon is a painter and mural artist trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera. She creates commissioned murals for private and professional clients between Monaco, Menton, the French Riviera, Liguria, and Milan. She speaks Italian, French, and English.
