How it works

If you write to me about a mural, the process stays simple and concrete.

1

Share your idea

Even if your idea is still a little undefined, we can shape it together.

2

Tell me about the space

Describe the atmosphere of the space. You can also send me a photo of the place.

3

Receive a first sketch

I’ll send you a first digital sketch based on a photo of your wall, to help you visualize the project more clearly.

4

Pricing and timeline

We’ll then decide together on the price and the date of the project.

Living with a painted wall

A painting that enters the home naturally

Here, the mural becomes part of the home. It can accompany an entrance, a living room, a bedroom, a staircase, or a ceiling, with a painting shaped for the space and for the life inside it.

Giulia creates murals for private interiors across Monaco, Menton, the French Riviera, Liguria, and Lombardy. Every project begins with the wall, the light, and the way the place is lived in.
