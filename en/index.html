About

My name is Giulia Dal Bon. I paint with everything I feel, and every project begins with listening : to the people, and to the light. I studied painting at the Brera Academy of Fine Arts, and since then, walls have become my favourite canvases. I create custom murals, portraits, landscapes and commissioned works for private homes, villas and professional spaces, between Monaco, Italy and France.

Hand-painted murals

Murals between Menton, Monaco, Milan, and the coast

Giulia Dal Bon creates custom murals for homes, villas, children’s rooms, hotels, restaurants, boutiques, wellness spaces, and professional interiors.

She works between Menton, Monaco, the French Riviera, Liguria, Milan, Segrate, Monza, and Lake Como, with travel depending on the project.
