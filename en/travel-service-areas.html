A clear base, an open range

Based between Menton and Milan

Giulia Dal Bon works between Menton and Milan, with frequent projects across the French Riviera, Liguria, and Lombardy. From there she also takes on projects in Monaco, Menton, Roquebrune Cap Martin, Saint Jean Cap Ferrat, Nice, Cannes, Portofino, Sanremo, Milan, Monza, Segrate, and Lake Como.

Even when a project calls for broader travel, the work still starts with the light, the volumes, the way the space is used, and the atmosphere you want.

Main base

Menton and Milan as two strong anchors in the work, between sea, city, private homes, and professional spaces.

Languages

English, French, and Italian for easier exchanges with clients, hotels, restaurants, boutiques, and design professionals.

Project based travel

Each trip is considered according to the place, the access, the time needed on site, and the scale of the commission.

Projects here often start more easily because Giulia already knows these places and these kinds of spaces.

Travel for selected commissions

Beyond the closest areas, Giulia can consider other places in France and Italy when the project truly fits her way of working. Homes, villas, hotels, restaurants, boutiques, wellness spaces, cultural venues, and projects led by architects or interior designers can also be considered outside the closest areas.

What matters most

The quality of the place, the wall surface, access, time needed on site, and the painting you want the space to hold.

Travel and planning

Travel costs and practical planning are discussed together in a simple, transparent way, according to the project.

First exchange

A photo of the wall, the city, a few approximate dimensions if you have them, and the atmosphere you imagine are enough to see whether the project can begin.
