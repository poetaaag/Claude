Questions that shape the project

A few concrete questions help clarify the room, the light, and the atmosphere you want from the start.

What kind of mural works well in Segrate ?

In Segrate, reception and meeting room designs, because demand comes mostly from companies and schools. Everything is painted by hand in acrylic, with no wallpaper and no printing.

Does it damage the wall ?

No damage. Acrylic bonds to a painted or plastered wall, with no stripping and no heavy undercoat. And if you change your mind in ten years, a roller of paint covers it.

When can we paint in Segrate ?

All year indoors. Outdoors, April to October, because of winter frost.

Can I see the design before saying yes ?

Yes. Before any agreement, Giulia shows you the project as an image on your own wall, with a detailed price. You change whatever you want, as many times as needed.

Ideas for spaces

Depending on the space in Segrate, the mural can stay simple, floral, figurative, decorative or more immersive, while remaining fully coherent with the architecture and the everyday rhythm of the place.

Houses and villas

Children’s rooms, kitchens, hallways, entrances, stairwells and ceilings. Example: a children’s bedroom of 8 sqm in a simple motif, around 1 200 €.

Hotels, restaurants and shops

Restaurants, cafes, shops, salons, showrooms, windows and commercial facades. In a market where competitors look alike, a mural is one of the few things that cannot be copied. Example: a corporate reception wall of 20 sqm in a detailed design, around 5 000 €.

Facades and exterior walls

Fog, frost and pollution soil facades. Washing and a fixative are needed before painting. For a facade visible from the street, the owner’s or the building association’s approval is usually enough, plus a notice to the town hall in a protected zone. From 150 € per sqm, plus scaffolding if needed.

Frequently asked questions about murals in Segrate

How much does a mural cost in Segrate ?

The most requested formats are listed above, starting at 450 €. A small mural in a bedroom costs less than a facade that needs preparation and scaffolding. The quote is free and without obligation.

How long does it take ?

A simple wall takes two to three days of painting. A full room or a very detailed design takes around a week. Add a few days for the sketch and approval.

Does a mural last outdoors in Segrate ?

Yes, with surface preparation and paints made for outdoor use. Same climate as Milan. Exterior work runs April to October, after washing the surface.

Is permission needed to paint a facade ?

For an interior wall, no. For a facade visible from the street, you usually need the owner's or the building association's approval, and a notice to the town hall if the building is in a protected area. Giulia explains the process before starting.

Can a mural be changed or removed later ?

Yes. A mural painted in acrylic can be covered like any wall paint. It can also be reworked or extended later.
