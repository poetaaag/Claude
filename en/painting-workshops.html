Frequently asked questions

Can I start from scratch?

Yes. Lessons and workshops can also be designed for someone who is just beginning.

Are lessons available in Menton, Monaco, or online?

Yes. Depending on the format, we can work online, in Menton, in Monaco, or in an agreed location.

Can I book for 2 people or more?

Yes. There are formats for duos, friends, family, and small private groups.

How do I book?

Just write to me directly with the idea, the location, and the format you want.
