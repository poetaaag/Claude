A mural approach shaped by this place

Beausoleil backs onto Monaco on a steep slope, with tightly packed buildings and public stairways between the streets. Blank gable walls and stairwells are plentiful and often bare. Demand comes mostly from building associations and the shops on avenue de Villaine, and from flats whose rooms lack light because of the slope. Giulia travels easily to paint: she comes on site to see the wall, or works first from photos and measurements.

Ideas for spaces

Depending on the space in Beausoleil, the mural can stay simple, floral, figurative, decorative or more immersive, while remaining fully coherent with the architecture and the everyday rhythm of the place.

Houses and villas

Children’s rooms, kitchens, hallways, entrances, stairwells and ceilings. Example: a living room wall of 8 sqm in a simple motif, around 1 200 €.

Hotels, restaurants and shops

Restaurants, cafes, shops, salons, showrooms, windows and commercial facades. In a market where competitors look alike, a mural is one of the few things that cannot be copied. Example: a building lobby of 12 sqm in a simple motif, around 1 800 €.

Facades and exterior walls

Sea air attacks facades. A salt-blocking primer is essential, with a UV-resistant finish, or colours fade within two summers. For a facade visible from the street, the owner’s or the building association’s approval is usually enough, plus a notice to the town hall in a protected zone. From 150 € per sqm, plus scaffolding if needed.

Frequently asked questions about murals in Beausoleil

How much does a mural cost in Beausoleil ?

The most requested formats are listed above, starting at 450 €. A small mural in a bedroom costs less than a facade that needs preparation and scaffolding. The quote is free and without obligation.

How long does it take ?

A simple wall takes two to three days of painting. A full room or a very detailed design takes around a week. Add a few days for the sketch and approval.

Does a mural last outdoors in Beausoleil ?

Yes, with surface preparation and paints made for outdoor use. Southern exposure and sea air, as in Monaco. Exterior work needs a salt-blocking primer and, for a wall visible from the street, a notice to the town hall.

Is permission needed to paint a facade ?

For an interior wall, no. For a facade visible from the street, you usually need the owner's or the building association's approval, and a notice to the town hall if the building is in a protected area. Giulia explains the process before starting.

Can a mural be changed or removed later ?

Yes. A mural painted in acrylic can be covered like any wall paint. It can also be reworked or extended later.
