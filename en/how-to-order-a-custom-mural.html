How to order a custom mural

Contact Giulia

You can write directly by WhatsApp or email. If you already have photos and measurements, that helps even more.

WhatsAppEmail

Start simply

What is useful to send right away

You do not need a perfect brief to begin. A few clear details are enough to understand the place, the wall, and the atmosphere you want.

Photos of the space

One or more images of the wall or room help show proportions, light, and the surface itself.

Approximate dimensions

Even a simple measurement is enough for an initial sense of the project.

Type of place

Home, villa, hotel, restaurant, boutique, showroom, facade, entryway, ceiling, or staircase.

The 6 details that truly help

1

Place

City, type of space, and the real use of the place.

2

Surface

Interior or exterior, condition of the wall, and any particular constraints.

3

Dimensions

Approximate scale, height, and surface area involved.

4

Atmosphere

Calm, poetic, bold, Mediterranean, figurative, floral, or something else.

5

Timing

If you have a precise window or an important date, it helps to say it immediately.

6

Budget

Budget is discussed through a custom quote, without a rigid public grid, depending on scale, site, and complexity.
