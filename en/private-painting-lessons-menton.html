Frequently asked questions

Where does the lesson take place?

In Menton, at home depending on the project, or in an agreed location.

Is it possible at home?

Yes, when the format and the place allow it.

Is it open to beginners?

Yes. We can start from the basics.

Can I book several lessons?

Yes. We can organize a single lesson or a small series.
